Gearhead Garage Builder

  1. Double-click Gearhead Garage Builder.
  2. The first time, macOS blocks it: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14 or older, right-click it and choose Open.
  3. Choose your game copy and click Build.
No game files are included. The Builder makes Gearhead Garage.app on your Mac from your own copy: the CD image, the disc, GEAR.MSI or an installed game folder.
