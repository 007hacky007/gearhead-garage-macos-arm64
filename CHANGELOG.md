# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release of Gearhead Garage Builder: makes Gearhead Garage.app on your Mac from your own copy of Snap-on Gearhead Garage (Build 131), from the CD image, the disc, GEAR.MSI or an installed game folder.
- The game runs as native ARM64 code with its full-color Direct3D mode drawn with Metal, the 256-color software mode, sound and music through CoreAudio, windowed or fullscreen.
